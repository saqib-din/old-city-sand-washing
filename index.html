About Us

OLD CITY SAND WASHING LLC is a Dubai-based recycling and construction materials company (License No: 901825), specializing in converting construction waste into black, white and red sands, road base and aggregates.

We are committed to sustainability, timely delivery and highest material standards across the UAE.

To lead the region in sustainable construction material recycling by delivering premium, eco-friendly sand products to support Dubai’s greener future.
